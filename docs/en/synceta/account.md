# Account & Workspace Management

Manage user authentication, team profiles, and workspace settings in SyncETA.

---

## 1. Registration & Verification

1. Click **'Create Account'** on the login screen.
   ![Login](./image/account/login_main.png)
2. Enter email and password, then click **'Send Verification Code'**.
   ![Send Code](./image/account/send_auth_code.png)
3. Enter the 6-digit code received via email to complete registration.
   ![Verify](./image/account/success_certify.png)
4. Set up your display name and avatar profile.
   ![Profile](./image/account/set_profile.png)

---

## 2. Profile & Preferences

Access **'My Profile'** to configure language preferences (Korean, English, Japanese), dark/light theme, and notification settings.

![Update Profile](./image/account/update_profile.png)
![Profile Form](./image/account/update_profile_form.png)
