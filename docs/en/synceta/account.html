Account ​

Sign Up ​

1. Click 'Create an account?' at the bottom of the SyncETA startup screen. ​

2. Enter your email and password, then click the 'Send Auth Code' button. ​

INFO

Sends an authentication code to the entered email.

3. Enter the email authentication code and click the 'Sign Up' button. ​

INFO

If the sign-up is completed successfully, it moves to the login screen.

4. Upon first login with the registered account, it moves to the 'Profile Settings' screen. ​

INFO

Please set up your profile picture and name.

Edit Profile ​

1. Click 'My Profile' at the top right of the main dashboard. ​

INFO

Clicking user settings moves you to the account settings screen.

2. You can edit your profile. ​

INFO
