Eliminate Spreadsheet Blind Spots in SI Project Margins and Statutory Compliance

In System Integration (SI) and enterprise software delivery, profitability hinges on reconciling Client Contract Billing Rates against Actual Input Costs (Internal Payroll, Freelancers, and Subcontractors) in real time. Relying on fragmented spreadsheets leads to unbilled milestones, bench resource waste, expired surety bonds, and statutory penalties.

SyncOps is an enterprise PSA (Professional Services Automation) and SI PMO platform purpose-built for high-compliance IT operations. It unifies Dual-Rate P&L pivot analytics, KOSA software engineer grade & M/M utilization matrices, 4-stage surety bond tracking, Subcontract Act 60-day payment monitoring, and SHA-256 timestamped R&D electronic lab notebooks.

5 Core Engineering Pillars of SyncOps

Protecting operating margins while enforcing 100% statutory and tax audit compliance.

1. Dual-Rate Real-Time P&L Pivot

Tracks Contract Billing Rates alongside Actual Resource Costs (internal salary, benefits, freelancer/subcontract payouts). Multi-dimensional pivot grids reveal real-time gross margin and operating profit margin (OPM) by project, client, and business unit.

2. KOSA Grade & M/M Utilization Matrix

Automatically evaluates software engineer grades based on credentials and project history. Visualizes 12-month rolling Man-Month (M/M) allocation matrices to prevent over-allocation, minimize bench time, and recommend optimal staffing via AI.

3. 4-Surety Bonds & Contract Governance

Manages the full lifecycle of Bid, Performance, Advance Payment, and Warranty surety bonds linked directly to prime/subcontract milestones and tax invoice issuance so collections are never delayed.

4. Subcontract Act 60-Day Compliance Guard

Monitors the statutory 60-day payment window from inspection/invoice date. Issues automated D-14 and D-7 alerts and calculates statutory overdue interest (15.5% p.a.) to eliminate regulatory risk.

5. SHA-256 Timestamped R&D Lab Notebooks

Anchors R&D institute engineering logs with author/reviewer digital signatures and SHA-256 cryptographic hash chains to guarantee tamper-proof evidence for R&D tax credit audits.

6. A2A Integration with SyncVerse & SyncInsight

Streams real-time margin erosion alerts and resource bottleneck metrics via MCP to SyncVerse and SyncInsight for executive simulation and Human-in-the-Loop approval.
