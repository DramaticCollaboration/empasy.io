Workflow Impact

How does it improve on traditional workflows?

Calmly control the biggest concerns in enterprise generative AI adoption—rising usage costs, single-provider outages, and sensitive data leaks—through a centralized governance layer.

Fragmented AI Integration

  • − Departments issue separate AI service accounts, making total spending and departmental attribution opaque.
  • − Customer phone numbers, IDs, or confidential details typed by staff risk being sent directly to external providers.
  • − An outage or slowdown at one AI vendor immediately stalls connected internal workflows.

With SyncLLM

  • ✓ Connect commercial AI services and private internal server models through one entry point with clear departmental budgets.
  • ✓ Reuse verified responses for recurring similar prompts and mask personal-data patterns automatically before transmission.
  • ✓ Fail over automatically to preconfigured backup models whenever a primary provider slows down or errors out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does connecting SyncLLM require heavy changes to our existing internal services?
No. SyncLLM supports standard AI connection endpoints, so most services integrate by updating only the connection URL and credentials. Technical details are available at doc.empasy.com/en/syncllm/.
Can we connect only our self-hosted internal models without external cloud AI?
Yes. You can connect private on-premises models exclusively and operate safely inside air-gapped networks.
What happens when a department reaches its budget limit?
Administrators can configure threshold alerts (such as 80% and 100%) or enforce soft/hard usage limits per policy.

Detailed architecture guides and integration specifications for engineering teams are available on our documentation portal.
