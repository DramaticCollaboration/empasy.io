Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the core role of the SyncVerse Central Control Tower?
SyncVerse serves as the single pane of glass and orchestration brain, harmonizing task dispatching, real-time telemetry, and inter-agent dependencies across all specialized SyncSeries agents (SyncEta, SyncBoot, SyncCMS, SyncCrawl, SyncInsight, SyncLLM).
How do Agent-to-Agent (A2A) messaging and Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrate?
Built on the open standard Model Context Protocol (MCP) and distributed A2A messaging buses, agents discover tools, exchange structured payloads, and resolve cross-domain operations dynamically without monolithic code coupling.
How does SyncVerse ensure safety via Emergency Kill-Switch and Human-in-the-Loop governance?
SyncVerse enforces cryptographic administrator approval (HITL) before applying code modifications or schema migrations. In case of unexpected anomalies, a single click triggers an Emergency Kill-Switch that locks the platform into ReadOnly status.
