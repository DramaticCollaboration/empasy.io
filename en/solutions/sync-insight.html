Workflow Impact

How does it improve on traditional workflows?

Eliminate back-and-forth engineering requests for routine aggregations and empower business users to explore operational data safely on their own.

Traditional Data Lookup

  • − Checking any filter missing from fixed dashboards requires waiting days for engineering or data teams to pull extracts.
  • − Non-technical staff struggle with query syntax and end up merging spreadsheet exports manually.
  • − It is hard to verify which source tables and calculations produced a reported KPI number.

With SyncInsight

  • ✓ Ask everyday questions like "Show last quarter's sales trend by category" and view live tables and charts immediately.
  • ✓ Read-only execution guarantees production operational data can never be accidentally modified or deleted.
  • ✓ Transparently displays both generated query criteria and data lineage graphs so analysts can verify every figure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Could a mistaken prompt alter or delete our production data?
No. SyncInsight enforces strict read-only querying and blocks any data-modifying or destructive commands at execution time.
How can we verify that AI-generated figures are accurate?
Alongside every chart, SyncInsight displays the exact underlying query conditions and raw result rows so owners can verify results directly.
Can we keep internal business data from leaving our network to external AI services?
Yes. You can deploy on-premises or combine SyncInsight with SyncLLM sensitive-data masking to prevent external data exposure.
