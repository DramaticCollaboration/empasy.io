Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How is SyncInsight different from traditional BI dashboards?
Traditional BI dashboards only display lagging historical charts. SyncInsight converts natural language questions into safe read-only SQL, correlates internal data with external market feeds, diagnoses root causes, and proposes executable business strategies.
Are AI-proposed strategies applied to production systems automatically?
No. SyncInsight always simulates expected revenue and margin impact first and queues the proposal for staged Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) approval before dispatching changes to operational systems.
How does SyncInsight protect sensitive enterprise databases during querying?
All connected target databases enforce strict Read-Only SQL guardrails that block DDL/DML mutations, combined with automatic PII masking and on-premise deployment support.
