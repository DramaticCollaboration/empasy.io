Workflow Impact

How does it improve on traditional workflows?

A complete commerce stack covering catalog management, order fulfillment and claims, flash deals, coupons, and a responsive buyer storefront.

Traditional Store Operations

  • − Calculating promotional discounts manually in spreadsheets often leads to accidental below-cost listings.
  • − Reverting hundreds of SKUs back to regular prices after a campaign ends or a mistake occurs is tedious.
  • − High promotional traffic slows down search, checkout, and admin operations simultaneously.

With SyncShop

  • ✓ Checks unit cost and minimum margin thresholds before applying price edits, catching below-margin mistakes upfront.
  • ✓ Records prior price states automatically on every bulk update so original prices can be restored cleanly at once.
  • ✓ Separates catalog, search, ordering, and admin services so peak campaign traffic stays smooth and responsive.

Margin Guardrails & Bulk Pricing Control

02

Price Snapshot Saving & One-Click Restore

03

Unified Order, Fulfillment & Promotion Ops

How It Works

STEP 01

Check Target SKUs & Margin Rules

Review target product groups, current inventory levels, and minimum margin thresholds before adjusting prices.

STEP 02

Snapshot Prices & Publish Campaign

Save previous price records automatically and apply new pricing and promotions to approved items.

STEP 03

Verify Storefront & Restore Post-Sale

Confirm live pricing on the buyer storefront and revert cleanly to saved prices once the campaign ends.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does SyncShop prevent accidental below-cost bulk price changes?
Minimum margin rules configured per SKU automatically catch price updates falling below thresholds and flag them for review before going live.
Does it include the customer-facing storefront as well as the admin console?
Yes. Both the operations admin console and a responsive desktop/mobile buyer storefront are included and ready to try.
Can we adopt SyncShop standalone without other Empasy modules?
Yes. SyncShop runs completely standalone and can later connect with SyncCMS or SyncInsight whenever needed.
