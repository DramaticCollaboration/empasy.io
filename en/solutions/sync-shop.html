Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can SyncShop integrate with our existing ERP and WMS?
Yes, SyncShop provides canonical RESTful APIs and MCP endpoints to seamlessly connect with enterprise ERPs, warehouse management systems, and payment gateways.
How are AI-driven price adjustments governed?
Strict margin guardrails and SyncVerse Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) approval policies ensure any price or discount change beyond defined thresholds requires explicit operator sign-off.
How does SyncShop handle high-volume flash sale traffic?
Because Gateway, Portal, Search, and Auth operate as independent microservices backed by Redis caching and message queues, high-traffic services scale horizontally without impacting back-office operations.
