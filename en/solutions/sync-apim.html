Workflow Impact

How does it improve on traditional workflows?

Automatically inventory sprawling service integrations, detect specification drift, and respond rapidly to unauthorized access and data leaks.

Traditional Integration Security

  • − Temporary or undocumented endpoints opened during development linger unnoticed, creating security blind spots.
  • − Sensitive customer fields accidentally included in responses go undetected when specs fall out of date.
  • − Investigating attack attempts requires sifting slowly through fragmented raw server logs.

With SyncAPIM

  • ✓ Analyzes live traffic automatically to discover and catalog undocumented shadow endpoints.
  • ✓ Compares official integration specifications against real responses to flag unexpected sensitive-field exposure.
  • ✓ Inspect detailed anomalous request payloads and apply blocking or masking policies immediately on one screen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will placing SyncAPIM in front of live services slow down response times?
You can begin with zero-impact mirrored inspection to map risks safely, then switch only critical routes to active blocking mode.
How does SyncAPIM differ from conventional web firewalls?
While traditional firewalls focus on known signature blocking, SyncAPIM specializes in integration security—automatic endpoint inventory, spec drift auditing, and response-level sensitive-data detection. See doc.empasy.com/en/syncapim/ for technical specs.
What is the benefit of pairing SyncAPIM with SyncWatch?
When SyncWatch organizes newly disclosed vulnerabilities, SyncAPIM lets your team apply corresponding inspection and blocking rules much faster.
