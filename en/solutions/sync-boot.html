Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does SyncBoot shorten enterprise back-office development from months to days?
SyncBoot comes pre-built with 71 standard enterprise screens and core modules—including Org/Role/Menu/Button dynamic RBAC, multi-stage electronic approval, data dictionaries, batch schedulers, and executive KPI dashboards—allowing engineering teams to focus solely on core business logic.
Can menu and button permissions be controlled dynamically per department and role?
Yes. Without any hardcoded routes in the frontend, SyncBoot dynamically provisions folders, views, and action buttons (Create, Edit, Delete, Export) from the database (`sys_permission`) and enforces department-level data visibility scopes.
Does SyncBoot provide full audit trail logging for security and compliance?
Every user login, role permission update, e-approval submission/sign-off, API call, and batch job execution is logged in real time with a unique Trace ID and execution duration for instant compliance verification.
