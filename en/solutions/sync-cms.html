Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can non-technical team members publish pages independently?
Yes, natural language commands and drag-and-drop live preview capabilities allow marketing and editorial teams to deploy pages without writing code.
How does multilingual localization work?
Editing primary content triggers automated contextual translation and synchronization across all configured target languages.
Can SyncCMS integrate with existing enterprise backends?
SyncCMS provides robust RESTful APIs and Spring Boot extensibility to connect with legacy databases, ERPs, and authentication providers (SSO/OAuth).
