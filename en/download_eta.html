Frequently Asked Questions

Is SyncETA desktop app free to download and run?

Yes. You can download installers for Windows and macOS directly from empasy.io to test internal and staging web services immediately.

How does SyncETA keep tests working when a page changes?

When button labels or page layouts change after a release, SyncETA analyzes the visible screen context to locate the matching target automatically and keep verification running.

Can SyncETA be used inside private corporate networks or VPNs?

Yes. SyncETA runs locally inside its built-in browser on your machine, allowing safe testing of internal company sites and VPN services without external cloud exposure.
