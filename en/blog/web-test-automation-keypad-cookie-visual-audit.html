Solving 4 Real-World Web Test Automation Blockers: Virtual Keypads, Cookie Popups, PII Masking & Visual Audits

3-Line Executive & Business Summary

Read Technical Breakdown of All 4 Blockers

1. Randomized Financial Virtual Security Keypads

Enterprise banking and public portals frequently render randomized on-screen keypads that block standard fill() inputs. SyncETA embeds virtual keypad detection directly in its recorder and runner so login verification remains automated without manual workarounds.

2. Late-Loading Global Cookie Consent Overlays

Third-party CMP banners often appear seconds after initial navigation. Instead of relying on fragile post-goto sleeps, SyncETA inspects cookie overlays immediately before DOM lookup inside its shared record runner, tracks per-tab completion in a WeakSet<Page>, and resets state strictly on main-frame framenavigated events—preventing both late-modal click interceptions and cross-tab interference.

3. Sensitive PII Exposure in Test Evidence

Automated screenshots and .webm failure videos can inadvertently capture national IDs or phone numbers. SyncETA masks sensitive fields across capture and LLM boundaries and runs locally on PCs or air-gapped on-premise servers with local LLMs.

4. Unscripted 404 Sub-Links and Text Overlap Defects

Functional scripts miss broken promotional links or overflowing multilingual copy outside scripted paths. SyncETA's site knowledge graph audits sub-links (404s and destination mismatches), text overlaps, blank regions, and broken media without pre-written scenarios—separating ambiguous captures into [Needs Review] for honest QA triage.
