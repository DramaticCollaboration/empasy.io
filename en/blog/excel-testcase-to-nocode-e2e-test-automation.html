1. Row-Selective Excel Conversion With Masked Datasets

Rather than discarding existing Excel/CSV test sheets, SyncETA lets QA teams upload spreadsheets, pick target rows (one row = one scenario), and attach structured test datasets per row. Variable placeholders such as ${keyword} are validated against dataset columns before generation, and only variable names—never live sensitive values—cross the LLM prompt boundary; real values are injected locally during browser execution.

2. Scaling Across Hundreds of Pages via Representative & Sample URLs

Global commerce and multi-product sites share page templates across dozens of locales. Defining one representative URL for scenario creation and binding additional sample URLs lets one validated test cover broad page sets without duplicating scripts.

3. Multi-Account Simultaneous Sessions & Honest [Needs Review] Triage

Cross-role workflows (such as customer submission in Tab 0 and administrator approval in Tab 1) run simultaneously in isolated browser sessions with synchronized video playback. Only verified checks count as passed; ambiguous screens are surfaced with screenshots under [Needs Review] rather than silently passing.

4. Photo-Backed Bug Kanban & One-Click [Check Now] Re-Verification

Detected defects generate deduplicated Kanban cards across Backlog, Open, Resolved, and Closed columns, remember permanent [Not a Bug] rules, and let QA verify fixes immediately with a single click on [Check Now]. During a 2–4 week pilot on one live site, teams measure weekly manual QA hours saved and pre-release defects caught directly.
