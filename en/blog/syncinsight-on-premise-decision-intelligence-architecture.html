Core operational data—ERP transactions, internal wikis, financial summaries, and market feeds—often sits isolated across departmental silos. Strict confidentiality rules prevent uploading these records to public cloud AI services, leaving business teams reliant on manual IT query queues.

Empasy's SyncInsight brings plain-language analysis inside your own servers or isolated VPC, combining structured database safety with citation-backed document retrieval.

1. On-Premise & Air-Gapped Security Architecture

SyncInsight supports deploying its PostgreSQL 18.6 pgvector store and local LLM runtimes (vLLM, Ollama) entirely inside private corporate networks—keeping sensitive records within your regulatory boundary.

2. Read-Only NL2SQL Firewall & Triple Hybrid RAG

  • Apache Calcite + JSqlParser SELECT-Only Firewall (sync-module-nl2sql): Parses every generated SQL statement at the AST level, blocking any write or DDL command (INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE, DROP) and restricting queries strictly to authorized read-only schemas.
  • Triple Hybrid RAG (sync-module-rag): Combines pgvector semantic search, BM25 keyword precision, and Knowledge Graph traversal across internal wikis and cleaned SyncCrawl market feeds.

4. Human-Approved Handoff to SyncVerse

Integrated directly into the SyncVerse console (/analytics/*), SyncInsight presents recommended follow-up actions alongside supporting evidence. Only after responsible managers review and explicitly approve an action does SyncVerse dispatch execution across downstream domain modules.
