[Tech Blog] Top-Level Decision Intelligence Penetrating On-Premise Security: SyncInsight's Multi-Agent Architecture

By Empasy Engineering Team

High-value information such as internal ERP, groupware, in-house Wiki, and market trend data are a company's core assets. However, indiscriminately utilizing large public cloud-based LLM services to analyze this data and derive strategies exposes companies to serious security regulatory risks of leaking sensitive corporate secrets and personal information. In addition, there was a limitation in deriving integrated insights because numerous data are thoroughly isolated (Silo) in departmental systems.

Empasy's SyncInsight goes beyond simple IT infrastructure automation to become the top-tier AI decision-making intelligence platform that drives flawless knowledge creation from internal assets and strategic business actions by C-Level executives. This article shares the core technical architecture of SyncInsight that derives powerful business insights from fragmented data while perfectly maintaining in-house security.

1. Hybrid On-Premise Architecture Based on 100% Internal Network

To fundamentally block data leakage risks, SyncInsight builds the entire environment, from data repositories to Large Language Model (LLM) serving servers, entirely within a 100% in-house private network (Intranet) or a dedicated isolated hybrid zone.

By completely blocking physical and logical transmission paths to the external cloud, it guarantees robust Data Sovereignty that 100% meets the strictest personal information protection laws and national security regulatory requirements in fields such as finance, public sector, and advanced manufacturing.

2. Context-Aware Advanced RAG Technology Layer

Simple keyword-matching embedding search engines have limitations in grasping the context of complex corporate documents. SyncInsight precisely applies an enterprise architecture based on the Spring AI and LangChain4j technology stack to operate a Context-Aware RAG pipeline that grasps the deep contextual meaning of the entire document.

It performs real-time cross-embedding of large-scale unstructured data fragmented across heterogeneous systems, including in-house wikis, groupware, ERP data, as well as external real-time market trend information collected by the SyncCrawl Agent. Through this, it permanently builds an ultra-high-quality knowledge data hub where the fatal limitation of generative AI, Hallucination, is completely controlled.

3. Strategic Autonomous Analysis via Multi-Agent Orchestration Loop

Within SyncInsight, an intelligent virtual dedicated team operates by dividing roles and collaborating on its own.

Collector Agent
Heterogeneous data collection
➔
Analyst Agent
Python-based big data analysis
➔
Reporter Agent
SWOT & strategy report generation
  • Collector Agent: Crawls and collects structured data from internal systems and external market indicators in real-time.
  • Analyst Agent: Autonomously executes Python scripts in a sandbox environment based on the collected raw data to perform large-scale statistical analysis and trend prediction.
  • Reporter Agent: Refines the analysis results and automatically generates visualization charts and SWOT strategic analysis report documents in a form that top decision-makers can grasp immediately within minutes.

4. Business Action Proposal & SyncVerse Feedback (Human-in-the-loop)

SyncInsight does not stay at the stage of simply showing static reports, but completes an organic feedback loop between system layers.

If crisis signs or new market opportunities are derived during the cross-analysis of internal and external data, it first proposes specific strategic actions to the management, such as "establishing a specific marketing promotion page and operating a target email crawling pipeline." As soon as management approves this in the system (Human-in-the-loop 1-Click approval), the top brain, SyncInsight, directly transmits a command protocol to the autonomous development/deployment belt of SyncVerse, the lower execution entity, achieving fully automated decision-making synergy that is immediately reflected on the system screen in real-time.

SyncInsight is the pinnacle of AI solutions that converts fragmented business data into real-time management assets while perfectly complying with corporate security guidelines. Through Empasy's unique autonomous operations intelligence, which connects data collection, analysis, and actual system application without any gaps, we will lead enterprise Digital Transformation (DX) to the next level.
