High-volume online stores update thousands of prices during seasonal sales and flash campaigns. Overlapping discount rules or manual entry errors can easily drop prices below purchase cost, creating direct financial losses and customer cancellations.

Built on a distributed 9-service microservice architecture (MSA), Empasy's SyncShop embeds Margin Guard validation and job-scoped snapshot rollback into every bulk pricing workflow.

2. Minimum Margin Protection (Margin Guard)

Even a uniform "20% off" campaign affects each SKU differently based on supplier cost and channel commission. SyncShop evaluates every candidate SKU against the projected margin formula prior to execution:

Projected Margin = (Proposed Price - Purchase Cost - Settlement Fee) / Proposed Price
  • Floor Filtering: Any SKU falling below its category threshold (e.g., 8% minimum margin) is isolated automatically and blocked from batch execution.
  • Pre-Execution Review: Displays exact counts of approved vs. excluded SKUs for manager sign-off before committing live price changes.

3. Pre-Change Snapshots & One-Click Rollback

  • Job-Scoped Snapshots: Records exact pre-update prices under a traceable Job ID before mutating pms_product.
  • Instant Recovery: If an external marketplace sync misbehaves or promotion terms shift, operators restore prior prices in one action using the Job ID.
  • Audit Accountability: Captures who approved the batch, when it ran, and which SKUs were skipped.

4. Chunked Updates Across Isolated Microservices

Updating tens of thousands of rows inside one monolithic lock degrades live customer checkout. SyncShop isolates admin batch processing from customer storefront services and commits updates in 500 to 1,000 SKU chunks, allowing targeted retries without disrupting live orders.
