Organizations in regulated industries accumulate vast volumes of unstructured business data every day—policy PDFs, meeting minutes, internal notices, partner guidelines, and regulatory updates. When these records remain scattered across folders and portals, employees spend hours searching for answers, yet feeding raw files straight into AI search introduces serious PII exposure and retrieval noise.

Empasy addresses this challenge with a 5-layer security-first data pipeline combining SyncCrawl, SyncLLM, sync-module-rag, and SyncInsight.

1. Three Common Bottlenecks in Enterprise Document Automation

  • Embedded Personal Data: Customer names, phone numbers, national IDs, account numbers, and emails often appear inside free-form documents, making unredacted indexing a compliance hazard.
  • Clutter & Broken Structure: Web menus, banners, repeated PDF headers, and flattened tables degrade embedding quality and cause misleading AI summaries.
  • Missing Access Controls & Stale Copies: Pooling every department's files into one flat vector index leaks restricted records or surfaces outdated policies.

2. The 5-Layer Security-First Data Structuring Pipeline

Every stage from ingestion to retrieval embeds validation, redaction, and auditability rather than bolting security on afterward.

LAYER 01
Compliant Collection & Diffing
SyncCrawl gathers approved public sources and internal repositories while skipping unchanged files via hash comparison
LAYER 02
Automatic PII Redaction
Detects and masks 8 categories of personal identifiers (IDs, phone numbers, accounts, emails) prior to embedding
LAYER 03
Structural Cleanup
Strips banners, navigation, and noisy markup into Clean Markdown/JSON while preserving headings, lists, and tables
LAYER 04
Triple Hybrid RAG Indexing
sync-module-rag chunks by semantic boundaries into PostgreSQL 18.6 pgvector + BM25 + Knowledge Graph
LAYER 05
Role-Scoped Retrieval & Audit
SyncInsight serves cited answers and charts strictly within department RBAC policies and logs all queries

3. Privacy & Private-Network Design Principles

  • Pre-Index Masking: Sensitive patterns are replaced with safe placeholders (such as [MASKED_PHONE]) before text ever reaches embedding or LLM inference layers.
  • Air-Gapped Execution Ready: The entire pipeline can run inside private servers using local embedding/LLM runtimes (vLLM, Ollama) and PostgreSQL 18.6 pgvector with zero external network egress.
  • Metadata-Enforced Governance: Every chunk carries document version, department ACL tags, and source citations so users only view authorized, up-to-date evidence.

4. Practical Rollout Guidance

  • Reduce Manual Sorting & Redaction Effort: Automating ingestion, deduplication, and PII masking lets staff focus on reviewing exceptions and approving high-value reports.
  • Start With High-Value Knowledge Sets: Begin with a 1-2 week pilot on 1-2 recurring document sets (such as internal policies or regulatory feeds) to validate masking accuracy before expanding enterprise-wide.
