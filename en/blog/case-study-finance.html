Detailed Project Case Studies by Sector (4 Cases · Anonymized per NDA)

In accordance with client confidentiality agreements (NDAs), organization names below are presented by industry sector, consistent with our Clients & Track Record page. Each case highlights the operational bottleneck, the technical approach, and observed field outcomes.

Education Platform · SyncETA

01. Large-Scale Automated Regression Verification for Digital Learning

2,000 concurrent users & 100 virtual classrooms validated prior to release

01. Challenge

Manually verifying attendance, live classroom interactions, and checkout flows took over 4 hours per release cycle, and traditional test scripts frequently broke after UI updates.

02. Solution (SyncETA)

Deployed parallel Playwright headless workers to replay peak interactive journeys, paired with self-healing selectors and automated issue-tracker reporting.

03. Outcome

Reduced pre-release verification from 4 hours to 45 minutes and caught visual and functional regressions reliably before production rollout.

IT Security & Finance Integration · SyncBoot · SyncAPIM

02. High-Volume API Security & Unified Monitoring Platform

Low-latency log search and anomaly governance under strict regulatory compliance

01. Challenge

Expanding external API integrations created surging traffic logs and required a high-availability platform to inspect anomalous access patterns rapidly across environments.

02. Solution (SyncBoot · SyncAPIM)

Built an active-active monitoring architecture combining the SyncBoot core with Redis and OpenSearch for rapid log indexing and centralized API policy governance.

03. Outcome

Delivered fast query visibility across massive access logs and established a compliance-ready foundation suitable for financial and public security audits.

Retail, Appliances & Materials · SyncBoot · SyncCMS

03. Legacy Back-Office Modernization & Global Multilingual Portal Consolidation

Streamlining 380+ legacy tables and unifying regional websites under one console

01. Challenge

Years of accumulated schema complexity and fragmented regional brand sites caused month-long delays whenever teams needed new operational screens or localized campaigns.

02. Solution (SyncBoot · SyncCMS)

Consolidated active data structures onto SyncBoot's standard RBAC and approval foundation while unifying multi-country web authoring and review flows in SyncCMS.

03. Outcome

Accelerated feature delivery from monthly to weekly cycles and empowered regional teams to publish multilingual pages safely under centralized governance.

Public & Education Administration · SyncInsight · SyncCrawl

04. Private RAG Knowledge Hub for 40,000+ Administrative Guidelines

Structured indexing and citation-backed retrieval inside private networks

01. Challenge

Over 40,000 administrative documents spread across thousands of affiliated institutions slowed policy lookup and created heavy repetitive inquiry workloads for staff.

02. Solution (SyncInsight · SyncCrawl)

Structured and indexed policy repositories inside a controlled private environment using hybrid RAG with mandatory source citations and drafting assistance.

03. Outcome

Cut repetitive lookup and drafting time significantly while providing verifiable, citation-backed responses aligned with administrative security policies.
