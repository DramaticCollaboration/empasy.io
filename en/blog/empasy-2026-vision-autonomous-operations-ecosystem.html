Hello, this is the Empasy Tech Blog.

The paradigm of software architecture is rapidly shifting from Monolithic to Microservices Architecture (MSA), and beyond Cloud Native to AI Agent-centric. Since its establishment, based on the firm philosophy of "Boon to Business by Agility," Empasy has been supplying high-performance solutions that allow enterprises to focus solely on the essence of their business without being hindered by technical complexity.

In 2026, Empasy declares a leap towards a 'Self-Evolving Business Ecosystem' that automates the entire coding, quality verification, and decision-making processes of enterprises by organically combining the powerful solution lineup built so far.

1. Empasy's Technological Base in 2026: 'Hyper-Connection' Beyond Fragmentation

The IT market up to now has been the heyday of fragmented AI tools solving problems in specific areas such as development acceleration (SyncBoot), content management (SyncCMS), and test automation (SyncETA). However, if each tool cannot share the context, a disconnection occurs where human developers must eventually copy data and give commands in between.

The core value Empasy sees for 2026 is the 'Connection of Context'. By fully embracing the global standard protocol MCP (Model Context Protocol), we have evolved all our solutions into 'Independent AI Agents' that judge and collaborate on their own.

  • Business Core Infrastructure (SyncBoot): Provides a standard MSA full-stack architecture that accelerates enterprise cloud-native transformation.
  • Frontend and Content Control (SyncCMS): Maintains an open CMS ecosystem visually assembled by business personnel without developer dependency.
  • Intelligent Quality Safety Net (SyncETA): Completes No-Code test automation that autonomously converts machine logs into human business language (Excel) and executes them.

2. Completion of Unmanned Autonomous Development Life Cycle: SyncVerse

SyncVerse, the central axis of Empasy's 2026 vision, is a central AI orchestration platform that connects the aforementioned core solutions like a single brain. When business personnel present requirements in natural language, it activates the 'Zero-Touch AI DLC (Development Life Cycle)' pipeline where the system evolves itself without humans coding or restarting servers.

Through this autonomous operations loop, it fundamentally blocks side effects that occur during the process of adding functions and refactoring large-scale enterprise systems, and dramatically shortens the lead time from idea to commercial service deployment.

3. Internalization of Top Decision-Making Intelligence: SyncInsight

At the top tier of the autonomous operations ecosystem lies SyncInsight, which features a 100% in-house internalized structure.

It integrates fragmented data assets between heterogeneous systems such as internal ERP, in-house Wiki, and external market trends (SyncCrawl) into knowledge using the Advanced RAG architecture. While perfectly meeting the stringent security regulations of financial and public institutions, it provides strategic reports in real-time to C-Level executives through multi-agent cross-analysis.

In particular, as soon as management approves the strategic business action derived by SyncInsight, command protocols are directly transmitted to SyncVerse, the lower execution entity, achieving flawless decision-making synergy where system development and marketing page deployment are linked in real time.
