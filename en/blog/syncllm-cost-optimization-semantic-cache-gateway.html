When multiple departments integrate external LLMs independently, predictable governance issues follow: fragmented API keys obscure total spend, semantically identical prompts trigger redundant charges, and confidential employee inputs risk leaving the corporate perimeter unmasked.

Empasy's central AI gateway, SyncLLM, unifies traffic between internal apps and external or local models behind four controlled stages.

2. Semantic Caching for Repeated Business Prompts

Traditional exact-match caches miss natural phrasing differences such as "How do I request leave?" vs. "Steps to submit annual vacation."

  • Cosine Similarity Matching: Embeds incoming queries and returns verified cached responses immediately when similarity exceeds admin-defined thresholds (e.g., 0.92).
  • Controlled Freshness: Supports per-category TTL policies and one-click cache flush whenever company rules or product terms change.

3. Tiered Model Routing & Zero-Downtime Failover

  • Lightweight Tasks (tier-2-balanced): Routes classification, extraction, and routine formatting to fast, economical or local models (vLLM, Ollama).
  • Complex Reasoning (tier-1-reasoning): Reserves flagship reasoning models for multi-step analysis, architecture design, and executive summaries.
  • Automatic Failover: Switches cleanly across OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, and private models whenever an upstream endpoint degrades.

4. Virtual Keys, Departmental Budgets & Security Testing

  • Encrypted Key Vault & Virtual Keys: Keeps provider secrets centralized while issuing departmental virtual keys with monthly budget limits and alert thresholds.
  • Built-In Red-Team Simulator: Tests prompt-injection and PII-masking guardrails proactively before policy changes roll out.
